What We Do

We make them and we lay them

Most people do one or the other. Because we manufacture our own pavers, you deal with one company from the batch to the broom finish — and the price doesn't carry a middleman.

Wetcast pavers produced in our own yard to SANS 1058:2021 — minimum 25 MPa, ±2 mm tolerance.

Installation

Contractors and developers buy direct from the yard. Consistent batches, predictable lead times, and volume pricing that holds through the job.
